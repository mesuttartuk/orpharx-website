Our focus

Focused on rare, orphan
and specialty medicines.

Our therapeutic focus reflects the areas where specialised pharmaceutical expertise, targeted commercial execution and a clear understanding of the healthcare environment are particularly important.

01

Rare diseases

Rare diseases represent a highly specialised area of healthcare, often characterised by complex diagnostic pathways, limited patient populations and concentrated treatment environments. Our interest in this field is driven by the opportunity to support selected therapies where focused pharmaceutical expertise and appropriate commercial execution can contribute to their development in Türkiye.

02

Orphan medicines

Orphan medicines are at the centre of our therapeutic focus. We are interested in selected therapies addressing rare and serious conditions, particularly where a focused commercial organisation can provide the product with the appropriate level of market understanding, distribution capability and commercial attention.

03

Specialty pharmaceuticals

Our focus also extends to selected specialty pharmaceuticals requiring specialised healthcare engagement, targeted commercial strategies and disciplined market development. We evaluate each opportunity according to its therapeutic characteristics, healthcare environment and strategic fit with OrphaRx.
