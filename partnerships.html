Partnerships

Building the right
partnership for Türkiye.

OrphaRx is open to discussions with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies seeking to develop the commercial potential of specialised medicines in the Turkish market.

A focused local commercial platform

We are currently establishing our portfolio and are actively evaluating selected products and partnership opportunities aligned with our therapeutic focus and commercial capabilities. Our objective is not to build a large portfolio, but to establish a focused portfolio of products where we can provide the appropriate level of attention, expertise and commercial execution.

What we look for

  • Rare diseases, orphan medicines and specialty pharmaceuticals
  • Differentiated therapies addressing significant medical needs
  • Products with a clearly defined treatment environment
  • Products requiring focused commercial development in Türkiye

Each opportunity is assessed individually, based on therapeutic relevance, product characteristics, market environment, commercial potential and strategic fit.
