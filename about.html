OrphaRx was established by pharmaceutical professionals with extensive experience across commercial strategy, business development, sales and marketing, market development and pharmaceutical operations.

Our structure is intentionally focused: rather than pursuing a broad portfolio, we aim to build a selective portfolio of specialised products where our experience and commercial capabilities can provide meaningful value.

OrphaRx is being built around this principle: focused expertise, informed decisions and disciplined execution.

A focused approach to specialised medicines
